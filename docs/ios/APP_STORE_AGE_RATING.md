# RooBin App Store Age Rating

Status: recommended App Store Connect answers  
Reviewed: 2 August 2026

## Recommended result

- Target global rating: **9+**
- Made for Kids: **No**
- Override to a higher rating: **Not applicable**
- Age suitability URL: leave blank for v1

RooBin contains no publisher-supplied objectionable content. The conservative
9+ target recognises that members of a private team can create fine names and
upload a team logo, so infrequent crude humour or profanity is possible. Do not
market RooBin as a children's app.

## Questionnaire answers

### In-app controls

| Question | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Parental controls | No | RooBin has no child-specific controls. |
| Age assurance | No | RooBin does not verify age. |

### Capabilities

| Question | Answer | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Unrestricted web access | No | Links open fixed RooBin policy/support pages in the system browser. |
| User-generated content | Yes | Members create team-scoped names, records and team logos. There is no public discovery feed. |
| Social media | No | No feed, follows, reactions, comments or public redistribution. |
| Social media disabled for users under 13 | Not applicable | No social-media capability. |
| Messaging and chat | No | Transactional invitations and security email are not user-to-user chat. |
| Advertising | No | No advertising or promotional network. |

### Mature themes

| Question | Frequency | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Profanity or crude humour | Infrequent | A private team can choose a humorous or crude fine name. |
| Horror or fear themes | None | Not part of RooBin. |
| Alcohol, tobacco or drug use/references | None | Not supplied or requested by RooBin. |

### Medical or wellness

| Question | Frequency |
|---|---|
| Medical or treatment information | None |
| Health or wellness topics | None |

### Sexuality or nudity

Set **None** for mature/suggestive themes, sexual content or nudity, and graphic
sexual content or nudity.

### Violence

Set **None** for cartoon/fantasy violence, realistic violence, prolonged
graphic/sadistic realistic violence, and guns or other weapons.

### Chance-based activities

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Gambling | No |
| Simulated gambling | None |
| Contests | None |
| Loot boxes | No |

Pool fixtures and the fines leaderboard do not place wagers, award prizes or
run a contest inside RooBin.

## Revalidation triggers

Re-run the questionnaire before shipping public team discovery, chat, social
features, advertisements, gambling/prediction features, or materially different
user-content capabilities.
