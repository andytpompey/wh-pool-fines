# RooBin commercial policy decisions

Recorded: 2 August 2026  
Decision owner: Andy Thomas

These decisions configure the initial RooBin Fines Team offer. They do not
replace professional legal or tax advice, and statutory rights always take
priority over product policy.

## Initial offer

- The captain purchases and accepts the terms on behalf of the team.
- The price is GBP 10 for one team and one named playing cycle.
- The Team offer does not renew automatically.
- A cancellation request prevents any future purchase; current access continues
  to its displayed expiry date.
- On expiry, users retain read-only access to their authorised historical data.
- No full or partial refund is normally provided for a period that has been
  used, except where required by law.
- RooBin is not VAT registered. No VAT is added and no VAT invoice is issued.
- The initial market is the United Kingdom.

## Discounts

No founding-league discount is hard-coded or advertised at launch. An
authorised platform administrator can create an audited, eligibility-scoped,
time-bounded fixed or percentage discount through Commercial Operations.

## Renewal and failed payments

Automatic renewal is not enabled for the Team offer. Recurring-payment retry
configuration is therefore not applicable to the initial launch. The catalogue
and lifecycle implementation remain available for a separately approved future
recurring offering.

## App Store route

The initial iOS route is an approved non-renewing StoreKit team-season purchase.
Web checkout uses Stripe and eligible wallets. App Review requirements must
still be checked at submission time.

## Refund policy draft for review

Customers keep access until the purchased period expires. RooBin does not
normally offer refunds or credits for a part-used period, a team choosing to
stop using the service, or unused time caused by a change of team plans. RooBin
will provide a refund, repair, replacement or other remedy where required by
applicable law. Suspected duplicate charges, unauthorised payments, material
service failure and provider disputes are reviewed individually. Nothing in
this policy limits statutory consumer rights.

This draft must be reviewed before support is authorised to issue or refuse
live refunds.

## Service availability wording

RooBin is operated with reasonable care but does not promise uninterrupted or
error-free availability or a particular uptime. Planned maintenance, urgent
security work, provider failures and events outside reasonable control may
interrupt access. Teams should keep independent copies of records needed for
time-critical administration. To the extent permitted by law, RooBin is not
responsible for indirect or consequential loss caused by temporary
unavailability. Nothing excludes liability that cannot legally be excluded or
limits statutory consumer rights.
